# Design System: Chi tiêu

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Bát men lam"** (the blue-and-white rice bowl)

The everyday Bát Tràng bowl with cobalt brushwork (*gốm men lam*) is in every Vietnamese kitchen: ordinary, trusted,
used three times a day, quietly beautiful. Chi tiêu borrows its material, not its motifs. Surfaces are cool porcelain
in light mode and a deep night glaze in dark mode; one cobalt voice (the *men lam*) carries the brand, the primary
action and the calm "you are fine" state; numerals are set heavy and large, like a confident brushstroke. Nothing is
decorated with pattern, crackle or ornament: the bowl is a palette and a temperament, never a costume.

The system is an **operate** surface. Density is moderate and the rhythm is generous: one column, wide gutters, strong
size and weight steps instead of boxes and borders. The dashboard leads with a single signature component, the
**status hero**, whose glaze changes colour with the money state (cobalt → butter → iron red → porcelain grey). That
change, animated once, is the only authored motion in the app; everything else uses quiet Material 3 transitions.
Warnings are plain and legible, never shouting: no full-saturation red, no flashing, no exclamation marks, and ordinary
spending is never coloured as an error.

Motion grammar: Material 3 duration and easing tokens; 150 ms for state feedback, 250 ms for list changes, 400 ms in
and 200 ms out for sheets; one 450-700 ms authored moment on the status hero; "Remove animations" collapses all of it
to 150 ms colour crossfades or instant cuts.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One cobalt accent (`primary`) on porcelain or night-glaze neutrals; colour strategy **Restrained**.
- Be Vietnam Pro throughout; money in 700-800 weights, proportional at rest, fixed-width digit cells where amounts form a
  column (list rows) and while the hero counts.
- Status hero with the *thước mức sàn* (floor ruler) as the reusable signature.
- Flat, tonal depth; shadows only on the FAB and floating overlays.
- Material 3 components and navigation, themed, never replaced.

## Colors

A cool porcelain-and-cobalt palette: one deep, slightly grey cobalt (*men lam*) on blue-tinted porcelain whites, a
celadon (*men ngọc*) tertiary for quiet system notes, and a status set tuned to read clearly in daylight without alarm.
Why this palette for this product: money anxiety is the problem the app solves, so the ground must feel as steady as a
plate you eat from every day; cobalt-on-porcelain is the most familiar "trustworthy object" in a Vietnamese home, and
it keeps red free for the moments that earn it (goal money in use, a failure, a destructive confirmation; see The Red
Is Earned Rule). It deliberately avoids the bank liveries the owner
already sees daily (Vietcombank green, MB Bank's bright blue and red), the old teal seed, and the AI-default looks
(cream + terracotta, purple-blue gradients, neon on near-black).

### Primary
- **Men lam cobalt** (light `#2C4B9A`, dark `#A6C1F9`): the OK status hero glaze, the "Thêm mục tiêu" FAB, the single
  filled button per screen or sheet, selected states, progress fills, links and text buttons, unread dots and badge
  counts. Its container (`#DBE6FE` / `#203A7E`) carries the OK hero in dark mode and the "Chưa phân loại" invitation
  pill.

### Secondary
- **Slate glaze** (light `#4C5B74`, dark `#B1BFD5`): low-emphasis tonal actions ("Gửi thử", "Đổi máy chủ / token"),
  the navigation indicator, selected filter chips and segments (`secondaryContainer`, a low-chroma slate kept visibly
  greyer and darker than `primaryContainer`), the "Nhập tay" FAB on Giao dịch, switch tracks when on, INFO alerts.

### Tertiary
- **Men ngọc celadon** (light `#2D6661`, dark `#98CDC7`): the demo banner, "Chuyển nội bộ" and next-month markers. Never
  used for money state and never in the same row as an `ok.foreground` amount (internal transfers show a neutral
  amount, see Money › Signed colours).

### Neutral
- **Porcelain** (`surface` `#F8FAFD`) and its tonal steps up to `surfaceContainerHighest` `#E4E7ED`; text in
  **Ink** (`onSurface` `#151A22`) and **Glaze grey** (`onSurfaceVariant` `#49505C`); field borders in `outline` `#737984`.
- **Night glaze** (dark `surface` `#11161F`, a blue ink, not black) stepping up to `#2B323D`; text `#E3E6EC` / `#B4BBC8`.

### Status (ThemeExtension `StatusColors`)

Four levels from `Level` (`ok`, `warning`, `danger`, `unknown`); `Level.info` maps to the secondary roles, not to a
status colour. Each level has: `foreground` (icons and short status text on any surface), `container` / `onContainer`
(pills, alert-row icon discs) and the hero set used only by the status hero: `heroBackground` (the glaze),

### Named Rules

**The One Cobalt Rule.** Cobalt `primary` fills appear only on the OK hero, the "Thêm mục tiêu" FAB and one filled
button per screen or sheet. Everything else that is cobalt is text, an icon, a thin progress fill, a field's focus
border, a dot or a badge count. Material defaults that would add cobalt fills are themed away: `Switch` tracks use
`secondary` (see Components), `Checkbox` and `Radio` are not used (switches and segmented buttons cover every choice),
and date/time pickers keep M3 defaults because their selected day is the one fill of that dialog.

**The Red Is Earned Rule.** `error` and `danger` colours mean exactly three things: goal money is being used (DANGER,
over-budget), something failed and needs fixing (form validation, a failed save, a failed connection step), or the
user is confirming a destructive action (the filled `error` button in a confirmation dialog and the `error` text button
that opens it). Outgoing transactions are `onSurface` with a "−" sign, "Chưa phân loại" is an invitation in primary,
offline and "could not refresh, showing cached data" are neutral strips. Never red.

**The Calm Badge Rule.** Unread counts, "chưa phân loại" counts and "new" markers use `primary` / `onPrimary`, never
`error`. A count is information, not an alarm.

**The No Bank Livery Rule.** No Vietcombank green, no MB Bank blue-and-red, no bank logos. Banks are named in text
("Vietcombank ··1234", "MB ··5678") on neutral avatars.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Be Vietnam Pro (bundled; no system fallback is expected to render)
**Body Font:** Be Vietnam Pro
**Label/Mono Font:** none (no monospace in the app)

**Character:** A Vietnamese-first geometric grotesque with open counters and well-built stacked diacritics; heavy
weights give the numerals a modern-bank confidence while 400-600 keep body copy friendly and calm.

Assets (`pubspec.yaml`, family `BeVietnamPro`): `BeVietnamPro-Regular.ttf` (400), `-Medium` (500), `-SemiBold` (600),

Every `TextStyle` sets `leadingDistribution: TextLeadingDistribution.even`.

### Money

Money has its own style family, applied through a `MoneyText` widget and `ThemeExtension MoneyStyles`, never by
hand-picking a `TextTheme` role.

| Money style | Numeral | Unit "đ" | Line height | Tracking | Digits | Use |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| `moneyHero` | 44sp / 800 | 26sp / 700 | 1.08 | -0.9sp | proportional; fixed cells while counting | Hero 'được tiêu' amount on the dashboard |
| `moneyLarge` | 28sp / 800 | 18sp / 700 | 1.15 | -0.4sp | proportional | Single amount heading a sheet (categorise sheet, alert detail) |
| `moneyInput` | 28sp / 800 | 18sp / 700 | 1.15 | -0.4sp | proportional | Text inside money fields (unit is the field suffix) |
| `moneyList` | 16sp / 700 | 16sp / 700 | 1.35 | 0.0sp | fixed cells | Trailing amounts in list rows (transactions, goals, accounts) |
| `moneyBody` | 16sp / 700 | 16sp / 700 | 1.5 | 0.1sp | proportional | Amounts inside bodyLarge sentences (forecast facts) |
| `moneyInline` | 14sp / 600 | 14sp / 600 | 1.43 | 0.0sp | proportional | Amounts inside captions, legend and progress lines |

- **Tabular figures are not in the font.** The Google Fonts build of Be Vietnam Pro has no `tnum` feature (checked: GSUB
  holds aalt, case, ccmp, dnom, frac, liga, locl, numr, ordn, sinf, ss01, subs, sups) and its digits are proportional
  ("1" is 0.446 em, "4" is 0.723 em at 800). `FontFeature.tabularFigures()` must not be relied on. `MoneyText` can
  lay digits out in fixed cells of the widest digit's advance (0.710 em at 400, 0.713 at 500, 0.716 at 600, 0.720 at
  700, 0.723 at 800), each digit centred in its cell, "." separators at their natural width. Cells cost space and
  rhythm: at 44 sp a "1" gains 12 dp (6 per side), so "1.111.111đ" is 262 dp in cells against 177 dp proportional, and
  "−12.500.000đ" (322 dp in cells, 303 dp proportional) scales down sooner. So cells are used only where they pay:
  - **`moneyList`, always:** trailing amounts in a list form a right-aligned column, and at 16 sp the cost is ≈ 2 dp
    per "1".
  - **`moneyHero`, only while counting:** the 550 ms count runs in cells so the number does not jitter, then settles to
    proportional digits with a 150 ms crossfade (see the authored moment). At rest the hero is proportional.
  - **Everything else is proportional:** single amounts (`moneyLarge`), text fields (`moneyInput`), sentences
    (`moneyBody`, `moneyInline`).
- **Open (owner):** shipping a modified Be Vietnam Pro with a real `tnum` feature (OFL allows it: the licence names no
  Reserved Font Name) would replace the cells everywhere, text fields included. Not part of the first build.
- **Format:** full digits, "." thousands separator, unit "đ" attached with no space (`4.000.000đ`); negatives and
  outgoing amounts use "−" (U+2212), incoming "+". The unit is set smaller (see table) and shares the numeral baseline.
  The font's "−" has a wide right side bearing (advance 661, xMax 545 per 1000 units) and the digits a 60-unit left
  bearing, about 0.18 em of air that reads as a detached sign in every money style, cells or not; `MoneySpans` closes
  it with a −0.1 em `letterSpacing` on the minus.
- **Signed colours:** outgoing `onSurface`; incoming `StatusColors.ok.foreground`; internal transfers between the
  owner's own accounts (*chuyển nội bộ*) `onSurfaceVariant` with their sign, because they are neither income nor
  spending; excluded (*đã loại trừ*) `onSurfaceVariant` with a strikethrough; pending (*đang chờ khớp*)
  `onSurfaceVariant`.
- **Hero fit:** `moneyHero` at 44 sp fits "4.000.000đ" (≈ 259 dp proportional) in the 288 dp the hero leaves on a 360 dp
  phone; longer values ("−12.500.000đ", 303 dp) scale down with `FittedBox(fit: BoxFit.scaleDown)`; the hero numeral
  clamps text scaling at 1.3× because it is already display size. Every other text keeps full system scaling.
- **Semantics:** wrap each amount in `Semantics(label: '<digits without separators> đồng')`, signed as "âm"/"cộng" when
  relevant, and exclude the per-digit children, so TalkBack reads "bốn triệu đồng" and not "4 chấm 000".

### Named Rules

**The Numeral Leads Rule.** A stand-alone amount comes first and its meaning second: the label sits under or after the
number, never as a small kicker above it. Inside a sentence the language decides the order ("Nên chi tối đa
~120.000đ mỗi ngày"). One exemption, by design: the status hero's heading ("An toàn", "Sắp chạm mức mục tiêu") sits
above the numeral, because it is not a label for the number but the answer to the first question the owner brings
("am I OK?"); it is a real heading with its level icon, at the hero's text weight, never a small uppercase kicker.
No eyebrows anywhere, no uppercase labels (Vietnamese diacritics stack badly in caps).

**The Exact Đồng Rule.** Money is never abbreviated in the app ("4tr", "4M", "4.000K" are out) and never rounded in the
hero, lists or forms. A "~" is allowed only where the server sends an estimate (forecast).

**The Diacritic Room Rule.** No wrapping role that can show user or bank text below 1.42 line height, no app-copy
heading below 1.25, no body text below 1.4, display roles never wrap, no fixed-height text boxes, and every string
tested with "Ễ", "ẫ", "Ộ" and an all-caps bank description at 200% font scale.

## Layout

One column, thumb-first. The answer (status and *được tiêu*) occupies the top of the first viewport; actions sit in the
bottom half (FAB, sticky sheet buttons); detail follows in the scroll.

**Spacing scale** (4 dp base, 8 dp rhythm):

| Token | s1 | s2 | s3 | s4 | s5 | s6 | s8 | s10 | s12 | s16 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| dp | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 | 24 | 32 | 40 | 48 | 64 |

- **Gutters:** 16 dp compact (< 600 dp), 24 dp medium (600-839 dp), 32 dp expanded (≥ 840 dp). Content column max
  720 dp, centred; bottom sheets max 640 dp wide.
- **Rhythm:** sections are headed in `titleLarge` (22 sp / 700); 32 dp between sections, 8 dp from a section heading
  to its content, 12 dp between sibling groups, 8 dp inline. More space above a heading than below it, always.
- **Rows:** 56 dp one-line, 72 dp two-line minimum (the standard money row has a meta line, so 72); 16 dp horizontal
  padding; 40 dp leading avatar or icon disc.
- **Money rows:** only the title shares its line with the trailing amount; the meta line (and pill, progress bar)
  runs the full text width under both. The text width inside a row is the window minus 2 × gutter, 2 × 16 dp row
  padding, the 40 dp disc and its 16 dp gap: 240 dp at 360, 270 dp at 390. "−1.250.000đ" in `moneyList` takes about
  113 dp at 100% (147 dp at 130%, 226 dp at 200%), so beside a 7-digit amount the title gets only about 111 dp at
  360 and 141 dp at 390 (the 224 dp in an earlier draft of this section was wrong). Titles may take 3 lines.
- **Large text and narrow rows:** a row switches to the stacked layout (title and meta full width, the amount on its
  own line under the meta, end-aligned so amounts still form a column) when the font scale is above 125%
  (`MediaQuery.textScalerOf(context).scale(16) > 20`), or when the room beside the amount is under 128 dp and the
  title would need more lines there than at full width (long bank descriptions at 360 dp; short goal and account
  titles stay beside their amount). Build these rows as one `MoneyRow` widget, not `ListTile.trailing` (which
  squeezes the title to zero width).
- **FAB clearance:** scrollables reserve 96 dp at the bottom so the last row is never under the FAB.
- **Touch:** every target ≥ 48 × 48 dp with ≥ 8 dp between targets (`materialTapTargetSize: padded`).
- **Edge-to-edge:** draw behind system bars; apply status-bar, navigation-bar, cutout and IME insets
  (`SafeArea`/`MediaQuery.paddingOf`); forms scroll the focused field above the keyboard.
- **System bars:** transparent status and navigation bars, `systemNavigationBarContrastEnforced: false`; icon
  brightness follows the theme: light theme dark icons (`Brightness.dark`), dark theme light icons. Set it once in
  `AppBarTheme.systemOverlayStyle` and in an `AnnotatedRegion<SystemUiOverlayStyle>` at the root (screens without an
  app bar, the first-run Connect screen and the demo strip included). The demo strip is `tertiaryContainer`, pale in
  light and deep in dark, so the theme's icon brightness is already right over it; nothing switches per screen.
- **Launch:** the Android 12+ splash uses `surface` as its background in each theme and the launcher icon; that icon
  does not exist yet (PRODUCT.md) and is a pending asset. Until it exists, debug builds may keep Flutter's default, but no
  release ships with it.
- **Adaptive:** compact and medium use `NavigationBar`; expanded (≥ 840 dp) uses `NavigationRail` with the FAB in its
  leading slot. At ≥ 1200 dp the dashboard may split into two panes (hero + forecast left at 400 dp, goals + accounts
  right); every other screen stays one centred column.

### Named Rules

**The First Glance Rule.** On a 360 × 640 dp phone the status heading, the *được tiêu* amount and the floor ruler are
fully visible without scrolling, with the app bar and the demo banner (when present) on screen. Checked by arithmetic
with the hero tokens (status bar 24, app bar 64, navigation bar 80 + 24 gesture inset, 8 above the hero; worst case a
two-line DANGER heading and a meaning line that wraps from 130%; the legend, context and freshness lines may fall below
the fold):

| Font scale | Hero top to ruler bottom | Room in the app | Room in the web demo (strip on two rows: 88 / 94 / 148 dp) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 100% | 180 dp | 440 dp | 352 dp |
| 130% | 240 dp | 440 dp | 346 dp |
| 200% (numeral clamped at 130%) | 350 dp | 440 dp | 292 dp: heading and numeral stay, the ruler scrolls |

The extended FAB (56 dp, 16 dp above the navigation bar, top edge at y = 464 dp) stays below the ruler in every row
above except the 200% demo case.

**The Proximity Before Boxes Rule.** Group by space first; add a container only when a group needs a shared surface
(a list of rows, the hero, the forecast). Never a container inside a container.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat and tonal. Depth comes from the surface ladder (`surface` → `surfaceContainerLow` → … → `Highest`), never from
shadows on content. Containers, list groups, the hero, the app bar (which only shifts to `surfaceContainer` when content
scrolls under it) and the navigation bar all sit at level 0 with `surfaceTintColor: Colors.transparent`. Shadows exist
only where an object genuinely floats above content and moves independently of it.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Level 3, 6 dp** (Material default shadow, `shadow` `#000000`): FAB, snackbar, dialog.
- **Level 2, 3 dp:** menus and dropdown overlays.
- **Level 1, 1 dp, no shadow:** modal bottom sheet (it sits on the scrim; the scrim provides the separation).

### Named Rules

**The Flat At Rest Rule.** Nothing that scrolls with the page casts a shadow. If a container needs to stand out, move
it one step up the tonal ladder instead.

## Shapes

Soft but decisive corners that grow with the importance of the object; stadium buttons; no outlines on containers.

| Token | none | xs | sm | md | lg | xl | xxl | full |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| dp | 0 | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 24 | 28 | 999 |

- `sm` 8 dp: chips, status pills, snackbars, badges. `md` 12 dp: text fields, small inline notes. `lg` 16 dp: list groups,

- Borders are reserved for inputs (`outline`, 1 dp; 2 dp `primary` on focus) and outlined buttons. Containers never

### Named Rules

**The Soft Bold Rule.** Radius scales with the container: 8 → 12 → 16 → 24 → 28. A small thing never gets a big radius
and the hero is always the roundest content object on screen.

- **Tonal:** `secondaryContainer` / `onSecondaryContainer` for secondary actions ("Gửi thử", "Đổi máy chủ / token").

  lại", "Ngắt kết nối"); the control that opens the dialog is a text button or icon button in `error`. This is the

### FAB "Thêm mục tiêu"
- `FloatingActionButton.extended`, icon `Icons.add`, label "Thêm mục tiêu"; `primary` / `onPrimary` (not
  `primaryContainer`: it is the product's primary action), radius `lg` 16 dp, 56 dp tall, level 3 shadow.
- Only on Tổng quan. Extended (the label is the affordance) at the top of the list and whenever the owner scrolls
  up; while scrolling down it collapses to a 56 dp icon FAB (tooltip and screen-reader label "Thêm mục tiêu") so it
  does not sit over the rows being read. On expanded layouts it moves into the `NavigationRail` leading slot and is
  shown only while Tổng quan is selected; other tabs keep its space empty (the rail does not jump) so Giao dịch never
  shows two FABs. Never disabled (a disabled FAB looks enabled in Flutter): when Tổng quan has no data at
  all (load error with no cache) the FAB is hidden and "Thử lại" is the screen's action; otherwise it stays enabled and
  the goal form's save-failure snackbar explains an offline save.
- Giao dịch has its own FAB, "Nhập tay" (`Icons.edit_note`): it is that screen's one creation action (categorising is
  done through rows, not a button), coloured `secondaryContainer` / `onSecondaryContainer` so cobalt stays unique to
  "Thêm mục tiêu". No other screen has a FAB.

### Chips
- **Filter chips** (Giao dịch "Chưa phân loại · 3", Cảnh báo "Chưa đọc"): 32 dp, radius `sm`, 1 dp `outline` stroke
  unselected (M3's default `outlineVariant` is 1.5:1 on `surface`, too faint to read as a control); selected
  `secondaryContainer` with a check icon, no stroke.
- **Choice chips** (category picker in the categorise sheet): the server's default categories (`system_key`) show a

  ("🎮 Giải trí"), as user data. The same rule applies everywhere a category is named: transaction rows and discs,
  goal meta lines (goals carry only the emoji, mapped back to the default category through the seed emoji), the
  category dropdowns. Selected: the check replaces the avatar (never drawn on top of it). 8 dp gaps between chips;

- **Status pill** (not a chip; non-interactive): 28 dp, radius `sm`, `StatusColors.<level>.container` with

### Lists and containers
- **List group:** `surfaceContainerLow`, radius `lg`, no border, no shadow, 4 dp vertical padding; rows inside are

  the only container used for lists; it never holds another filled container.
- **Row anatomy:** 40 dp leading disc (`surfaceContainerHighest` with an `onSurfaceVariant` icon, or a status container
  disc for alerts), `titleMedium` primary text (up to 3 lines), `bodySmall` `onSurfaceVariant` meta line, trailing
  `moneyList` amount right-aligned on the title's line; the meta runs the full text width under title and amount; at
  most one status pill, under the meta. Rows with an amount are `MoneyRow`s and follow the money-row rules in Layout.
  Plain rows (settings, alerts) may be `ListTile`s; a multi-line `ListTile` aligns its leading icon to the top.
- **Account row** (one component, used on Tổng quan and in Cài đặt): leading disc with the bank short name in text
  ("VCB", "MB") or `payments_outlined` for cash; title "Vietcombank ··1234"; meta "cập nhật 14:32" (+ "ước tính",
  "không tính vào tổng"); trailing `moneyList` balance or "chưa biết số dư" in `bodyMedium` `onSurfaceVariant`; the
  "Chưa xác nhận" pill (warning container) under the meta for auto-discovered accounts. No chevron. Tap opens the account sheets
  (`surfaces/account-sheet/brief.md`).
- **Inline notes** (e.g. "Đã có mục tiêu khác cùng danh mục…") are an 18 dp icon plus `bodySmall` text inside the row;
  never a nested tinted box.
- **Not allowed:** cards inside cards, coloured left/right borders, eyebrow labels above headings, same-size icon cards
  as page structure.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `OutlineInputBorder`, radius `md` 12 dp, 1 dp `outline`, 56 dp tall, persistent floating label,
  `bodyLarge` text; helper text `bodySmall` `onSurfaceVariant`, written to fit 3 lines at 100% but never truncated
  (`helperMaxLines` / `errorMaxLines` 8, for 200%). Floating labels stay short ("Phần trăm mức sàn", "Đã chi trước khi
  dùng app"); the explanation goes in the helper. Supporting text under a non-field control (the type segmented
  button) is indented 16 dp like field helpers. Non-money numbers (percent, day of month) use `titleMedium` in the
  64 dp field height of the money field beside them, not `moneyInput`.
- **Focus:** 2 dp `primary` border, label in `primary`. **Error:** 2 dp `error` border, message in `error` below
  ("Nhập số tiền", "Số tiền phải lớn hơn 0"). **Disabled:** 38% `onSurface`.
- **Money fields:** 64 dp tall, `moneyInput` text (proportional digits), suffix "đ", `TextInputType.number`, live grouping with
  `VndInputFormatter`; placeholder is an example ("VD: 3.000.000"), never the label.
- **Segmented buttons** for two-way choices, with short labels that fit half of a 328 dp row at 130% ("Quỹ giữ" /
  "Ngân sách", "Tháng 10" / "Tháng 11", "Khoản chi" / "Khoản thu"); the full term goes in the helper line under it
  ("Ngân sách chi tiêu: hạn mức chi trong tháng…"). Flutter splits the width equally (each segment ≤ 164 dp at 360 dp),
  so when a label plus icon and padding does not fit (measured with `TextPainter`, e.g. "Tháng 1/2027" at 130%, any
  label at 200%), the control switches to `direction: Axis.vertical` with full-width segments. Selected:
  `secondaryContainer` with the check icon.
- **Switches:** `Switch` list tiles for booleans, with a one-line consequence as subtitle. Themed, not default:
  selected track `secondary`, thumb `onSecondary` (M3's default selected track is `primary`, which would put a cobalt
  fill on five screens); unselected track `surfaceContainerHighest`, thumb and border `outline`. Behaviour follows the
  context: in Cài đặt (a preferences screen) a switch takes effect at once and confirms with a snackbar; inside a
  create/edit form or sheet ("Lặp lại hằng tháng", "Tính cho tháng sau", "Tính vào tổng số dư") it is part of that form
  and commits with its save button.

### Navigation
- **Compact / medium:** `NavigationBar`, 80 dp, `surfaceContainer`, no shadow, indicator `secondaryContainer`, filled
  icon for the selected destination, labels always shown in `labelSmall` (12 sp / 600): Tổng quan (`dashboard`), Giao
  dịch (`receipt_long`), Cảnh báo (`notifications`), Cài đặt (`settings`). Flutter clamps these labels at 130%; "Tổng
  quan" then measures 86 dp in a 90 dp slot (it would be 92 dp at 13 sp). The Cảnh báo badge is themed through
  `badgeTheme` to `primary` / `onPrimary` (Flutter's default `Badge` colour is `error`), "99+" cap.
- **Expanded (≥ 840 dp wide and ≥ 480 dp tall):** `NavigationRail`, `labelType: all`, top-aligned, FAB "Thêm mục
  tiêu" as `leading` (Tổng quan only, see FAB). A landscape phone (wide but under 480 dp tall) keeps the
  `NavigationBar`: the rail does not scroll and at large text would push "Cài đặt" off screen.
- **Top app bar:** small M3 app bar, `surface`, title `titleLarge`, start-aligned; `scrolledUnderElevation: 0` with
  `surfaceContainer` as the scrolled-under colour. Icon buttons always carry a Vietnamese `tooltip`. On wide windows the
  tab's app bar spans the whole area beside the rail (so the scrolled-under tint is not a slab over the centre
  column); its title and actions align with the 720 dp content column (gutter + side inset; trailing text/icon buttons
  end at the content edge), and the tab's FAB and snackbars stay inside the column.
- **Back:** system Back and predictive Back always work (`PredictiveBackPageTransitionsBuilder` on Android); sheets
  close on Back before the route pops. On Giao dịch, Cảnh báo or Cài đặt, Back first returns to Tổng quan (the start
  destination); only Back on Tổng quan leaves the app.
- **Screen readers and keys:** each row in a list group is its own semantics node (a question row is read before its
  own answer buttons); the Cảnh báo badge is read as "N cảnh báo chưa đọc" after the tab name; sheet scrims read
  "Đóng"; Tab moves through a screen's content before its FAB and the navigation bar.

### Bottom sheets
- Modal, `showDragHandle`, `isScrollControlled`, `useSafeArea`, max 88% height and 640 dp width, `surfaceContainerLow`,
  top radius `xxl` 28 dp, 20 dp side padding, scrim `scrim` at 32%.
- The sheet's primary action is a full-width filled button in a sticky bottom bar that rides above the keyboard.
- Used for: categorise transaction, alert detail, account add/edit and balance entry
  (`surfaces/account-sheet/brief.md`). Dialogs only for destructive confirmations.

### Snackbars
- Floating, `inverseSurface` / `onInverseSurface`, radius `sm`, action text `inversePrimary`, above the FAB and
  navigation bar; 4 s, 6 s with an action. Confirmations are short and offer undo where the API allows it
  ("Đã phân loại" · "Hoàn tác"). Persistent problems never live in a snackbar; they stay inline.

### Status hero (signature)
The first object on Tổng quan; one container, radius `xl` 24 dp, 20 dp padding (`heroPadding`), full content width,
colours from `StatusColors.<level>.hero*`: OK the cobalt glaze (`primary` in light, `primaryContainer` in dark),
WARNING butter (light) / umber (dark), DANGER iron red, UNKNOWN porcelain grey (light) / slate (dark). Every text,
icon and the ruler tick inside it is `heroForeground`.

Anatomy, top to bottom, with the spacing tokens between parts:
1. **Status heading:** 24 dp level icon + 8 dp + `Level.label` in `titleMedium` at 700, wrapping (the DANGER label,
   275 dp, takes two lines in the 256 dp it has at 360 dp). It is the hero's heading (semantics header), exempt from
   the Numeral Leads Rule for the reason given there. → `heroHeadingToNumeral` 4 dp.
2. **Numeral:** `free` in `moneyHero` (44 sp / 800, unit "đ" 26 sp / 700), proportional digits at rest, signed with
   "−" when negative. → `heroNumeralToMeaning` 4 dp.
3. **Meaning line:** `titleSmall` 600, one line per state that adds information instead of repeating the heading:
   - OK: "được tiêu thoải mái · còn 17 ngày";
   - WARNING: "dưới vùng đệm 600.000đ · còn 17 ngày" (says why a positive amount is a warning);
   - WARNING while waiting for salary (`waiting_income`, `free` may be negative): "đang chờ lương ngày 5 · lương về sẽ đủ";
   - DANGER: "thiếu cho mục tiêu · còn 17 ngày" (the numeral is the signed shortfall, e.g. "−350.000đ").
   "· còn N ngày" is left out when `days_left` is missing. → `heroMeaningToRuler` 16 dp.
4. **Thước mức sàn (floor ruler):** a 12 dp bar (`rulerBarHeight`) with rounded ends; segments are separated by 2 dp
   gaps of `heroBackground` (`rulerSegmentGap`). Domain = max(balance, floor + buffer).
   - Reserved segment [0, min(balance, floor)] in `heroBarReserved`; free segment [floor, balance] in `heroBarFree`.
   - **Tick** at floor + buffer (the warning line): 2 × 20 dp in `heroForeground`, centred on the bar so 4 dp show above
     and below. Wherever it crosses a segment it is cut out of it with a 2 dp `heroBackground` halo on each side
     (`rulerTickHalo`): the free segment is the same colour as the tick, so without the halo the tick would vanish in
     OK, the most common state (1.00:1; see the appendix).
   - WARNING: [balance, floor + buffer] is left blank, so the free segment visibly stops short of the tick.
   - DANGER and WARNING-waiting-for-salary (balance < floor): the reserved segment is [0, balance]; the missing part
     [balance, floor] is an empty segment outlined with a 1.5 dp `heroBarReserved` stroke (`rulerMissingStroke`);
     [floor, floor + buffer] is blank and the tick closes it.
   - No goals (floor = 0): one free segment and no tick (without goals the server keeps the level at OK, so there is
     no warning line to show).
   - One semantics node: "Số dư 10.000.000 đồng, giữ cho mục tiêu 6.000.000 đồng, được tiêu 4.000.000 đồng, báo khi
     được tiêu dưới 600.000 đồng". → `heroRulerToLegend` 12 dp.
5. **Legend, two rows** (`heroLegendRowGap` 4 dp): label at the start in `bodySmall`, amount end-aligned in
   `moneyInline`: "Giữ cho mục tiêu … 6.000.000đ" and "Số dư … 10.000.000đ", with " · ước tính" after "Số dư" when any
   counted balance is derived. Each row wraps: when label and amount do not fit on one line the amount drops under the
   label, end-aligned. Measured at 360 dp (288 dp inside the hero): row 1 needs 210 dp at 100% and 268 dp at 130%;
   row 2 with "ước tính" 206 / 264 dp; both wrap at 200%. (A single left/right row would need 345 dp.)
   → `heroBlockGap` 12 dp.
6. **Context lines (only when true),** `bodySmall`, `heroLineGap` 4 dp apart: "Đang chuyển giữa các TK: 2.000.000đ"
   (`in_transit`), "Lương dự kiến chưa về: 15.000.000đ (ngày 5)" (pending salary, when the meaning line does not already
   say so). The server's `level_reason` is not printed in the hero: it restates the heading and the numeral ("Đã lấn
   350.000đ vào tiền dành cho mục tiêu"); it remains the text of Telegram messages and alert rows. → `heroBlockGap` 12 dp.
7. **Freshness line (last):** `bodySmall` "Cập nhật lúc 14:32"; stale (> 48 h) becomes "Số dư chưa cập nhật 2 ngày"
   with `Icons.schedule` 16 dp at 600 weight; offline adds "Đang offline · số liệu lúc 14:32" with `Icons.cloud_off`.
   Always `heroForeground`: the hero never recolours a line to signal staleness.
8. **UNKNOWN:** the heading "Chưa biết số dư" (step 1) stays and is not repeated; steps 2-6 are replaced by one
   `bodyMedium` sentence and, 16 dp below, one filled button: no accounts → "Thêm tài khoản và nhập số dư hiện tại để
   app tính được phần được tiêu." + "Thêm tài khoản"; accounts without a balance → "Nhập số dư hiện tại của
   Vietcombank ··1234 để app tính được phần được tiêu." + "Nhập số dư" (`enter_balance`). Both open the account sheets
   (`surfaces/account-sheet/brief.md`).

**The authored moment (status change).** Two triggers, both on refresh, push or resume and never on first paint:
- **Amount or level changed** (`summary.free` or `summary.level` differs from what the hero last showed): the glaze
  tweens through `StatusColors.lerp` over 450 ms (`Curves.easeInOutCubicEmphasized`); the numeral counts from old to new
  over 550 ms (emphasized decelerate `Cubic(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1)`) in fixed digit cells, inside a `FittedBox` whose
  scale is frozen at the smaller of the start and end scales, so gaining a digit or crossing zero (the "−" appears)
  never re-scales the number mid-count; at 550 ms it crossfades over 150 ms to the proportional rendering of the final
  value. Ruler segments resize over 500 ms (emphasized decelerate); the ruler fades in or out over 150 ms when entering
  or leaving UNKNOWN.
- **Level changed** (only then): the status heading leaves 8 dp upward and fades over 150 ms (emphasized accelerate),
  the new one rises 8 dp over 250 ms after a 100 ms delay (emphasized decelerate), and a live region announces
  "Trạng thái: <label>. Được tiêu <số> đồng." A 30 s refresh that only moves the amount re-counts quietly and does not
  interrupt TalkBack.

Total ≤ 700 ms, the same choreography for better and worse states, no shake, pulse, flash or haptic.
**Reduce motion** (`MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf`): 150 ms colour crossfade; numeral (proportional), heading and
ruler change instantly. **Loading:** a static skeleton in `surfaceContainerHigh` (heading bar, numeral block at 60%
width × 44 dp, ruler); no shimmer, no spinner.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** read every colour from `ColorScheme`, `StatusColors` or `MoneyStyles`; keep all hex values in
  `mobile/lib/theme/`.
- **Do** keep cobalt fills to the OK hero, the "Thêm mục tiêu" FAB and one filled button per screen (The One Cobalt Rule).
- **Do** show money exactly: `4.000.000đ`, "−"/"+" signs, fixed digit cells for list columns and the hero count only.
- **Do** pair every status colour with its icon and Vietnamese label; colour is never the only signal.
- **Do** put freshness next to every balance ("cập nhật lúc 14:32") and say *ước tính* / *đang chờ khớp* plainly.
- **Do** use bottom sheets for in-context tasks and dialogs only for destructive confirmations.
- **Do** verify light and dark, 130% and 200% font scale at 360 dp width, and "Remove animations" before calling a
  screen done.

### Don't:
- **Don't** colour outgoing transactions, offline or refresh-failed strips, or "chưa phân loại" red (The Red Is Earned
  Rule).
- **Don't** leave Material defaults that add cobalt or red where the rules say otherwise: theme `Switch`, `Badge` and
  `FilterChip` strokes, and never disable a FAB (hide it instead).
- **Don't** use eyebrows, uppercase labels, section numbers, cards inside cards or coloured left/right borders.
- **Don't** use `ColorScheme.fromSeed`, Dynamic Color, `Colors.*` or `Color(0x…)` outside `mobile/lib/theme/`.
- **Don't** abbreviate or round money ("4tr", "4M"), and don't rely on `FontFeature.tabularFigures()` with this font.
- **Don't** use emoji or Unicode arrows as UI icons (today's "↑"/"↓" avatars, "🎯" goal badge and "💵" cash avatar go);
  user category emoji stay.
- **Don't** add shadows to content, gradients, glass/blur, glow, or any bank's colours or logos.
- **Don't** animate anything on load except skeleton-to-content; the status change is the only authored motion.
